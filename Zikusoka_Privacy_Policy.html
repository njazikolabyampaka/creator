01 / ABOUT THE GAME

Who we are

Zikusoka is a Tower of Hanoi logic puzzle game developed and published by NJAZIKOLA, a development initiative of KEIGA Foundation, based in Kampala, Uganda. This policy describes the privacy practices of the Zikusoka mobile application.

Offline gameplayNo internet connection needed to play

No permissionsNo access to sensitive device features requested
02 / YOUR INFORMATION

Information we collect

We do not collect personal data through Zikusoka. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or other personal details.

We do not use in-app analytics, advertising identifiers, behavioral tracking, crash-reporting services, or profiling technologies. We do not sell or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

Gameplay state, including your current moves and puzzle position, is handled within the app on your device while you play. It is not sent to NJAZIKOLA or to an external server.

03 / OFFLINE BY DESIGN

Internet and permissions

Zikusoka's core gameplay works entirely offline. You do not need a network connection, account, subscription, or login to play.

The app is designed not to request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, files, or other sensitive device resources. It does not require permission prompts to play.

04 / EXTERNAL SERVICES

Third-party services

Zikusoka does not integrate advertising networks, social sign-in, third-party analytics, or external tracking SDKs. The game does not send gameplay or personal information to third-party services.

If you install or update Zikusoka through Google Play, Google may independently process information as part of operating its store, download, security, or device services. That processing is governed by Google's own policies and is separate from data collection by Zikusoka. You can read Google's Privacy Policy for details.

05 / AGE SUITABILITY

Children and teenagers

Zikusoka is intended for players aged 13 and above. It contains logic puzzles suitable for teenagers and adults.

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about the app's privacy practices, please contact us using the email below.

Age suitability described here is not a substitute for the official Google Play content rating or target-audience declarations, which are determined separately during store submission.

06 / DATA HANDLING

Security and retention

Because Zikusoka does not collect or transmit personal information, NJAZIKOLA does not maintain a database of player information or retain user records collected through the app.

The puzzle operates on your device. Information handled temporarily during gameplay remains within the application environment and is not uploaded to our servers. Device and operating-system security remain important for protecting your device generally.

07 / YOUR CONTROL

Your choices

You can play without providing personal information. There is no account to delete, no marketing subscription to cancel, and no in-app data collection to opt out of.

You may uninstall Zikusoka at any time. If you have a privacy question or wish to inquire about information associated with a direct email you have sent to NJAZIKOLA, contact us at the address below. Email correspondence is separate from the Zikusoka app and is used to respond to your inquiry.

08 / UPDATES

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy if Zikusoka's features, privacy practices, or applicable legal requirements change. Any revised policy will be published with a new effective date. If future versions introduce data collection or third-party services, we will update this policy and any applicable Google Play disclosures before those changes are released.
