01 / Our purpose

Potential is everywhere.
Opportunity should be too.

Njazikola is the innovation and software development department of KEIGA Foundation. Born from a commitment to uplift young people in village communities, we help emerging talent develop practical life skills, build confidence with technology, and create pathways to economic independence. We believe the next generation of changemakers deserves more than a chance to learn — they deserve a chance to lead.
